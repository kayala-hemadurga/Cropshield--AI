# Cropshield--AI
CropShield AI 🌱

Predict. Protect. Prevent.

CropShield AI is an AI-powered agricultural crop threat monitoring and early-warning platform designed to help farmers monitor crop health, report potential threats, and make informed decisions.

🌾 Key Features

- Crop Health Scanner: Upload crop images for crop-health assessment.
- Threat Monitoring: Review field reports and potential crop threats.
- Early-Warning System: Understand risk factors and recommended next steps.
- Community Reports: Record crop observations to help identify possible threat clusters.
- Farmer-Friendly Interface: Simple and accessible design for agricultural users.

🎯 Goal

Help farmers identify potential crop problems earlier, understand risks, and seek appropriate expert guidance.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Crop risk indicators and image assessments depend on the data and AI services available in the application. Potential threat clusters are not confirmed outbreaks. Farmers should seek qualified agricultural advice when diagnosis is uncertain.
